## 3. `both`/seed=2024 stopped mid-training by request

This is the 12th and final run of the Qwen QAT matrix. Training was
progressing normally -- loss in a 3.2-4.8 range, clipping engaging on
essentially every step (consistent with the 100% clip-rate finding from
Day 9), no spikes or NaN at any point observed -- when the user asked to
stop for the day and push findings to GitHub.

The stop was issued as a task-kill, not a graceful `Ctrl+C`/`SIGTERM` the
training loop's interrupt handler is designed to catch (see
`train_qat.py`'s `interrupted` flag and its immediate resume-checkpoint
save on signal). The log confirms this directly: it ends with `[killed]`,
not the `Interrupted at step N/500 -- saved resume checkpoint` line the
graceful path would have printed. That meant recovery depended on the
periodic checkpoint (`config.CHECKPOINT_EVERY_STEPS=10`) rather than an
up-to-the-second save.

Verified directly rather than assumed: loaded
`checkpoints/RESUME_qwen2.5-0.5b_both_seed2024.pt` and read its `step`
field back out -- `250`. The process had reached step 253 in the log
before being killed, so the real cost of this stop is **3 steps
(~410s of compute)**, not the up-to-30-step window `CHECKPOINT_EVERY_STEPS`
could in principle have cost. Training will resume from step 250/500 next
session.
