## 2. Direct assessment requested: time estimate, methodology soundness, gaps

Asked directly for an honest read on three things. Answering honestly here
rather than reassuringly:

### Time estimate

At the observed pace (350/500 steps in 5.1 hours), finishing just this one
run would take **~7-8 hours total**. Whether the other 11 Qwen runs behave
the same way is unknown -- Pythia's slowdown (Day 2/3) was *intermittent*,
not universal, so there's real uncertainty in both directions:
- **Best case**: this is an anomaly specific to this run/session, and
  subsequent runs return to the ~20-45 minute norm. Remaining matrix:
  perhaps 6-10 more hours.
- **Worst case**: the slowdown is now the norm for Qwen specifically.
  Remaining matrix: potentially 2-3+ days.

This is a genuine unknown, not a hedge -- the pipeline's performance
characteristics on this specific model are not understood well enough to
forecast confidently, which is itself worth stating plainly rather than
picking a number that sounds authoritative.

### Is the methodology sound?

Yes, on the core experimental design:
- Multi-model architectural diversity (OPT/Pythia/Qwen) directly answers
  the "does this generalize" reviewer critique, and has already produced a
  genuinely interesting, non-obvious result (activation-quantization
  sensitivity varies by ~30x between OPT and Pythia).
- The FP16 fine-tuned control correctly isolates domain adaptation from the
  quantization effect -- this was a real methodological fix from early in
  the project (v1's original mistake), and it's held up under scrutiny.
- 3-seed variance is real, measured data now, not a single anecdote.
- The memory-scaling law (docs/MATH.md) is empirically fitted and validated
  against 2 real models, not asserted from a rough estimate.

## 3. Improvement points identified this session (not yet acted on)

1. **Sanity-check slow-run correctness, not just speed.** Before trusting
   any perplexity number from an anomalously slow run, verify it against an
   independent quick recomputation (e.g. re-evaluate the resulting
   checkpoint's perplexity on a small subset immediately) to rule out the
   slowdown being a symptom of something computing incorrectly rather than
   just computing slowly.
2. **Decide on parallelization of remaining work rather than strict
   sequencing.** Given Qwen's matrix alone may take days, it may be more
   productive to start Section B (QLoRA frontier) and add per-example NLL
   logging for Section D now, in parallel with -- rather than strictly
   after -- Qwen's matrix finishing. This wasn't decided this session; it's
   flagged as a real choice to make, not a default to fall into.
3. **Investigate whether the slowdown correlates with anything
   measurable** (time of day / other processes on the laptop / thermal
   state) rather than continuing to treat it as unexplained noise -- no
   data has been gathered specifically aimed at this yet, only anecdotal
   GPU-utilization checks that rule out a hang but don't explain the slowness.
